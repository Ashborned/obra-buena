## Full description

```
Soul Shelter is a Catholic prayer app that begins with a simple question: how are you feeling today?

FROM HOW YOU FEEL
Choose from twelve feelings, such as tired, happy, lonely, angry or grateful. For each one you will find a Bible verse, a short prayer and a saint to keep you company. A different entry appears each day.
If you choose sad, depression or lonely, the app shows you how to reach help in your country.

TODAY
The saint of the day in a stained-glass window, the day's liturgical color, the Gospel and the novena you are praying.

NOVENAS ON REAL DATES
Each novena is prayed on the nine days before the saint's feast, on its real calendar dates. Light a candle for each day you pray and, if you like, get a reminder.

LEARN
Short readings about the lives of the saints, gathered in collections. As you read you discover their traits, and at the end you can answer a few questions to earn their medal. The game lives only here, in what you learn: prayer is never scored.

PRIVATE AND AD-FREE
- No account, no sign-up.
- Everything you do is stored only on your phone. Nothing about what you feel or pray leaves it.
- No ads and no analytics.
- Works offline.

YOUR WAY
English and Spanish, two color palettes, adjustable text size in the readings, support for your phone's large text, and an option to reduce animations.

FREE
Soul Shelter is free, with nothing locked. If you wish, you can support its development with a voluntary contribution that unlocks nothing.

Quoted Bible texts are in the public domain.
```
